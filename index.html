Built around a real payment challenge

Maldivians primarily earn and spend in MVR while many global digital-asset and cross-border payment systems are centered on foreign currencies. MVX is exploring how blockchain infrastructure could connect familiar MVR-denominated payments with modern digital settlement without positioning the asset as a speculative investment.

Accessible

A simple payment experience designed to hide unnecessary blockchain complexity.

Low-cost

Designed around efficient settlement for transfers and merchant payments.

Compliance-first

Regulatory, KYC/AML, reserve and consumer-protection requirements are part of the design process.

How MVX is intended to work

The initial MVP will be a testnet demonstration and will not accept real customer funds.

1
Deposit workflow
Prototype how approved MVR payment channels could initiate issuance.
2
Receive MVX
Demonstrate an experimental MVX asset on Stellar Testnet.
3
Pay & transfer
Test wallet-to-wallet transfers and merchant QR payment flows.
4
Redemption workflow
Prototype the process for redeeming the digital asset through compliant infrastructure.

Planned Stellar integration

MVX plans to use Stellar as the settlement infrastructure for the MVP. Stellar SDKs/APIs would be used to create and submit transactions, retrieve balances and transaction history, and demonstrate fast, low-cost settlement on testnet.

Production launch is conditional. A live MVR-linked MVX asset would only be considered after appropriate regulatory, reserve, banking, security and compliance arrangements have been established. MVX is not presented as an official currency or as being endorsed by the Maldives Monetary Authority or the Government of Maldives.
